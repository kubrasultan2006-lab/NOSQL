- Cardinality: 4,931 distinct values; the most frequent value holds 2.65% of the orders.
- Writes: new inserts spread to 1.08x the ideal load on the busiest shard (shares: 24%, 25%, 27%, 24%).
- Data: 1.12x imbalance across shards for all stored orders.
- Queries: 50% of the workload is routed to a single shard; the average query touches 2.50 of 4 shards.
- Trade-off: these queries become scatter-gather: Get one order by order_id (4.0 shards); Orders placed in a 7-day range (reports) (4.0 shards); Orders by status (no key field) (4.0 shards).
- Trade-off: a hashed key gives up efficient range queries on customer_id, which this workload does not need.
- Ranking of the others: customer_id (2.70), order_id (2.30), order_date (1.70).
